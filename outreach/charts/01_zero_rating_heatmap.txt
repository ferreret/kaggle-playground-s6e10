Heatmap of the share of satisfied airline passengers by the score (0 to 5) they gave to each of 13 services, in 699,635 synthetic training rows; the overall rate is 44.4%. Scores 4 and 5 are blue (more satisfied than average) for almost every service, and scores 1 to 3 are mostly red. The highlight is the 0 column: for the digital services a 0 goes with high satisfaction — 89% for inflight wifi (against 39%, 27% and 28% for scores 1 to 3), 71% for online booking and 62% for online boarding — while for services everybody uses, such as seat comfort or cleanliness, a 0 is rare (0.1% of rows) and behaves like a 1.
